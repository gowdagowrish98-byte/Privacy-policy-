Nutri Lens Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Nutri Lens ("the app") lets you scan packaged food barcodes and see a nutrition score. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Camera

The app uses your camera only to read barcodes. Images are processed on your device and are not stored or uploaded.

Barcode lookups

When you scan or type a barcode, the number is sent to Open Food Facts (a public food database) and to our cloud database to fetch product details and healthier suggestions. We do not send your name, email or any account information. Like any internet service, these providers may see technical data such as your IP address.

History and favourites

Your scan history and favourites are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. Clearing the app's data deletes them.

Accounts, ads and sale of data

The app has no user accounts and shows no ads. We do not sell your data.

Sharing

Share cards are created on your device and are shared only if you choose to share them.

Not medical advice

The Nutri Lens Score is for general education. It is not a medical diagnosis or a government-approved rating.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
